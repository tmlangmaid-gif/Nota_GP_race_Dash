Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and a hashed (bcrypt) form of your password.
  • Race data you create: events, vehicle numbers, driver names and colours, lap times, lap notes, tyre stints, team memberships and invites.
  • Server logs: short-lived runtime logs from the scraper and the API, used for debugging.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, no advertising IDs, no third-party tracking pixels.
  • No payment-card details — those go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers.
  • No location data beyond what's inherent in the timing data you choose to track.

Where data is stored

On a Hostinger VPS in a Postgres database, behind a Let's Encrypt HTTPS certificate served by Traefik. Backups are not regular and not guaranteed — see the data-retention disclaimer in the Terms above.

Third parties we send data to

  • Stripe — when you pay for an event. Stripe receives your email and the event metadata required to process the payment. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Resend — when we send transactional email (password resets, team-invite notifications). Resend receives the recipient email and message contents. See Resend's privacy policy.
  • Natsoft (or other live-timing sources) — we read publicly-available timing pages on your behalf. We don't send your personal information to them.

Cookies / local storage

We don't use tracking cookies. The site stores your auth token and your light/dark theme preference in your browser's localStorage so you stay logged in and the site looks the way you set it.

Your data, your call

  • You can change your email or password at any time in Account settings.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Account settings. Deletion removes your account and every event you own, including all the lap data attached to those events.
  • If you'd like a copy of the data tied to your account, contact the operator (see below) and we'll do our best within the limits of this hobbyist setup.

Race Dash isn't directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has registered, contact the operator and we'll delete the account.
