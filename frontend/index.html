How to find the live URL: click Browse Natsoft… → in the popup, click Circuit Racing → click Live next to your meeting → when the live timing appears, press F12 → Console tab → paste this single line and hit Enter: 
copy(document.querySelector('iframe[src*="LiveMeeting"]').src)
That copies the right URL. Come back here, click Paste URL, then Create. Or use demo:// for synthetic data.
